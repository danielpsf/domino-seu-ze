# Rules

Brazilian block dominoes (dominó de bloqueio), two players.

## Setup

- Double-six set: 28 tiles.
- Each player gets 7 tiles. The other 14 sleep face down and are not used (no drawing).

## Play

- The first hand opens with the highest double in play, usually the 6-6 (carroça de sena). With no double dealt, the heaviest tile opens.
- After that, whoever won the previous hand starts.
- On your turn, place a tile so one of its numbers matches an open end of the line.
- If you can't play, you pass.

## End of a hand

- **Batida:** a player places their last tile and wins the hand.
- **Jogo trancado:** both players pass in a row. The hand with the lower pip total wins. A tie means nobody wins the hand.

## Scoring (bar rules)

- Batida: 1 point.
- Batida de carroça (the last tile is a double): 2 points.
- Jogo trancado: 1 point to the lighter hand, 0 on a tie.
- First player to 6 points wins the match.

Lá-e-lô and cruzada score no bonus here; they count as a normal batida or a batida de carroça.

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Carroça | A double, like 4-4 |
| Bater | To play your last tile |
| Passar | To pass |
| Trancar | When nobody can play |
| Dorme | The unused tiles |
